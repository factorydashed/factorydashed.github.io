lov-ed

A retro for your relationship. Every few weeks, sit together with one phone and take turns: thanks, what went unsaid, sorry, small pacts and questions.

Coming to Google PlayApp Store later
  • Works offline
  • No account
  • Doesn't record what you say
  • Android first
  • No network permission

The retro

Last time

See how your pacts and your last good time went.

Check-in

How connected do you feel? Each picks 1 to 5.

Grateful

Thank each other, and say what you're grateful for in life.

Unspoken

Say what went unsaid, starting with "I felt", never "you did".

Words for feelings

Find the feeling, then what you need, with a live sentence to say aloud.

Repair

A way to say sorry, and reply ideas for the one listening.

Pact

Up to 3 small pacts: "After I ___, I will ___".

Feeling loved

"What can I do to make you feel more loved?" Small acts, not money.

Good time

Plan one good time before the next retro: light, nice or big.

Ask me anything

Draw a question: fun, deep, dreams, memories, now or silly.

Close

Did you feel heard? Then a hug and the date of the next retro.

Between retros

Home

Take turns

One talks, one listens. Swap any time.

Resume

Pick up an unfinished retro where you left it.

Reminder

One gentle reminder on retro day.

Names, how often, reminder time and language.
